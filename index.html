Technical FAQs

Before you request a quote.

What is a custom plastic extrusion profile?

A continuous plastic component made through a custom die. Its cross-section stays consistent along the length, making the process suitable for channels, trims, seals, pipes, tubes and strips.

Can ASMCO manufacture from a physical sample?

Yes. A sample, dimensioned sketch or technical drawing can start feasibility review. Final tooling requires agreed dimensions, tolerances, material and working conditions.

Do you manufacture injection-moulded parts?

ASMCO specializes in continuous extrusion. Parts with changing geometry or deep three-dimensional forms normally require injection moulding and may not suit our process.

Which plastic materials can be extruded?

Material depends on the application. Common options include rigid and flexible PVC, PE, PP and other extrusion-grade polymers, subject to geometry and performance requirements.

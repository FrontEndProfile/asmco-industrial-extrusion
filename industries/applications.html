B2B application guide

One extrusion process. Hundreds of product uses.

The opportunity is rarely “a plastic profile.” It is a functional part inside a machine, panel, bed, cabinet, light, display or building system.

Electrical & switchgear

Cable trunking, wiring ducts, busbar insulation, panel edge trims, DIN-rail covers, spacers and enclosure seals.

LED & lighting

Diffusers, lens covers, mounting channels, light strips, decorative trims and wire-routing profiles.

Furniture & laboratories

Worktop edging, drawer guides, shelf trims, glass channels, panel jointers, bumpers and protective feet.

Medical & hospital equipment

Soft tubing, protective rail inserts, cable management, edge guards and continuous components for beds and trolleys.

Doors, windows & glass

Glazing beads, weather seals, glass channels, screen tracks, joining strips and finishing profiles.

Ceiling & wall systems

PVC ceiling jointers, corner profiles, shadow gaps, panel trims, decorative strips and wall cable covers.

Refrigeration

Door gaskets, shelf strips, glass guides, breaker profiles, spacers and protective trims.

Retail displays

Price label holders, shelf data strips, sign rails, dividers, ticket strips and bumper profiles.

Industrial machinery

Wear strips, conveyor guides, belt profiles, rollers, guards, slide rails, sleeves and machine edge protection.

Automotive support parts

Protective channels, wiring sleeves, flexible trims, seals and non-safety-critical continuous components.

Packaging equipment

Guide rails, wear strips, product lanes, protective covers and low-friction continuous components.

Agriculture & fluid handling

Small-bore pipes, flexible tubes, protective sleeves and routing components subject to material suitability.
